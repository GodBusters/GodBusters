How to Play

MoveDrag anywhere on the screen to steer. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys.
Fight automaticallyYour weapons strike on their own. Focus on dodging and positioning.
Level upCollect gems from fallen monsters. Each level lets you pick a new weapon or blessing. Carry up to four weapons.
Gold & amphoraeSmash amphorae for figs, gold, the Horn of Hermes or the Wrath of Zeus. Spend gold in the Temple.
Survive until dawnNight deepens for ten minutes. Titans arrive at 3:00, 6:00 and 9:00. Live to see the sunrise to win.
